Welcome to my website!

I am a **Ph.D. scholar in Economics** in the Department of Liberal Arts at the **Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH)**, India. My research lies at the intersection of **development economics, gender, and political economy**, with a particular focus on the **causal impact of internet access on women’s attitudes and behavior** in India.

My doctoral research examines how digital connectivity shapes women’s agency, social norms, and decision-making, using applied microeconometric methods and large-scale household data.

I completed my **Master’s in Population Studies and Health Economics** from the **Gokhale Institute of Politics and Economics, Pune** in 2023, and earned my **Bachelor’s degree in Economics** from the **University of Calcutta** in 2021.
